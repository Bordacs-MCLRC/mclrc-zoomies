How to play

Run, jump and splash through each act. Collect biscuits to fill the Zoomies meter, bounce on robot hoovers, and sniff out hidden gold rosettes.

Keyboard

  • ← → or A D run
  • Space or ↑ jump
  • ↓ sniff (stand still) or dive
  • Shift Zoomies
  • Esc or P pause

Touch

  • Arrow buttons to run
  • Jump button to jump
  • Nose button to sniff or dive
  • Lightning button for Zoomies
  • Easy mode: tap anywhere to jump

Gamepad

  • Stick or D-pad to run
  • A to jump
  • Down to sniff or dive
  • B or X for Zoomies
  • Start to pause
